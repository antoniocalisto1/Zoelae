Shown only to the people you choose.

Before you hand anything over

Who can see my collection?

Nobody, until you make a shared link. A link shows only the works you put in it and only the fields you switched on, to someone who has the passcode, until it expires or you revoke it. There's no public profile and nothing for a search engine to find.

What does it cost?

Nothing. {{SITE}} is free to use, with no plans to choose and no card to enter. If that ever changes you will hear well before, and you can take everything out at any point.

Can I add a whole folder of photographs?

Yes. Choose a folder, or drag it in, and every photograph becomes a work waiting for its details. Then go through them one by one: title, artist, year, medium, size, price. Anything you skip waits for you in the collection.

What can I upload?

Photographs as JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 25 MB each, plus certificates, invoices and condition reports as PDFs or images. Each photo is turned the right way up, cleaned of location and camera data, and given a smaller copy so your collection loads quickly.

Can I get everything back out?

Yes. One click in your account downloads a single file with every record, every image at full size and every document, in a folder for each work, with a spreadsheet of the whole catalogue.

What if I forget my password?

Ask for a reset link on the sign-in page. It's emailed to you, works once, and expires after an hour. Changing your password signs you out everywhere else.

What happens when I delete my account?

Your records, images, documents and shared links are removed straight away, and every shared link stops working. Nothing is kept for later.
